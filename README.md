# hmmmMeme

One click, one random meme.

**Live:** https://hmmmeme.useful-indeed.com

hmmmMeme is a one-button break from your day: click and get a random meme. Click again for another one. There's no feed to scroll, no account and no algorithm learning about you — just a quick laugh when you need it.

## How to use

1. Open the page — a meme loads automatically.
2. Click the meme, press Next, or hit Space / → on your keyboard for another one.
3. Like one? Use Copy link to share it with a friend.

## Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing about you is stored. The “memes seen” counter lives only in the open tab.
